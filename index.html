First Generation Units
  • Royal units' names are followed by an asterisk.
  • Corrin can use a Partner Seal to inherit a class from any unit with whom (s)he has achieved A rank.
  • Nohr Prince(ss), Wolfskin, Kitsune, Songstress, and Villager cannot be inherited through Partner or Friendship Seals.
  • Same-sex pairings do not result in children.
  • If the unit has the Aptitude skill equipped, each stat's growth rate in game will be 10 higher than the value shown as the growth rate here.
  • Pair up bonuses shown are for S Rank partners only.
  • In addition to the pair up bonuses listed, Outlaws, Adventurers, Ninjas, Master Ninjas, and mounted promoted classes provide +1 movement.

Child Units
  • Mods shown when no second parent is selected assume the second parent will not be a child.
  • Classes from the second parent are always inherited first.
  • Royal units' names are followed by an asterisk.
  • Though the option will still be provided here, sibling units cannot S rank with each other.
  • Nohr Prince(ss), Wolfskin, Kitsune, and Villager can only be inherited from a parent.
  • Songstress cannot be inherited under any circumstances.
  • Base growth rates are independent of second parent and class.
  • Individual growth rates are calculated based on the selected second parent but do not consider the selected class.
  • If the unit has the Aptitude skill equipped, each stat's growth rate in game will be 10 higher than the value shown as the growth rate here.
  • Pair up bonuses shown are for S Rank partners only.
